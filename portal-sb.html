    FAQ

    Common questions.

    Which boards and institutions does Shaalix support?

    CBSE, State, ICSE, IB, Cambridge and Montessori schools, as well as preschools and colleges.

    Can we move our existing data from Excel?

    Yes. During setup we migrate your student, staff and fee records from Excel sheets.

    How do parents stay informed?

    Through the Android and iOS parent app, plus WhatsApp and SMS alerts, in Telugu, Hindi or English.

    How is Shaalix priced?

    Pricing is per student, so it scales with your school. Book a demo and we will share a quote for your institution.

    Can we run several branches on one account?

    Yes. Each branch keeps its own data, and management sees combined reports across the group.

    Which tools does Shaalix connect to?

    Zoom, Google Meet and Google Workspace for classes; LeadSquared and Meritto for admissions; Tally, Razorpay and UPI for fees; and biometric and RFID devices for attendance.
